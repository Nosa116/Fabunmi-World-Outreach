Apostolic Fellowship & Doctrine

Apostolic Alliance Fellowship of Nigeria

Apostolic Doctrine TLBC Bible College Holiness & Fellowship

APOSTOLIC ALLIANCE FELLOWSHIP OF NIGERIA®, as the name implies, was born out of the burden to rescue the deteriorating, reckless, and individualistic kingdom-building orientation that has become the order of the day in Christian circles, done at the detriment of the mindset of God for His church.

"Thus saith the Lord, stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls."

— Jeremiah 6:16
Our Biblical Beliefs
1. The One True and Living God

Maker of all creation; eternal Father, who manifested Himself in His Son, Jesus Christ, for redemption, and as the Holy Ghost. (Deut 6:4; 2 Cor 13:14; 1 Tim 3:16).

2. Divine Inspiration of Scriptures

The original Old and New Testament scriptures are the inspired Word of God and the sufficient rule of faith, practice, and final authority. (2 Tim 3:16; 2 Peter 1:20-21).

3. The Gospel of Jesus Christ

Virgin birth, sinless life, sacrificial death on the cross, triumphant bodily resurrection, and exaltation as King of kings and Lord of lords. (Acts 10:38-40; 1 Cor 15:1-4; Col 2:9).

4. The Apostolic Plan of Salvation

Proclaimed on Pentecost: faith in Jesus, repentance, water baptism by immersion in the name of Jesus Christ, and receiving the Holy Ghost. (Acts 2:1-39; 10:44-48).

5. Life of Holiness & Genuine Christianity

Living inward and outward holiness through grace; love for God and people, prayer, obedience, worship, family commitment, honesty, and diligence. (Eph 4:21-24; Heb 12:14; Rom 12-13).

6. The Church, Second Coming & Life Everlasting

The universal body of Christ operating in spiritual power; the glorious appearing of our Lord; righteous reward and everlasting life. (1 Cor 12; 1 Thess 4:13-18; Rev 20-21).

Global Chaplaincy & Service

Excellent Chaplaincy Global Mission (ECGM)

RC: 8452727 Institutional Chaplaincy Peace & Crisis Deployment

A registered voluntary organization committed to training and deploying chaplains globally to deliver spiritual care, promote peace, and maintain ethical standards in service to God, Government, and Humanity.

Deploying chaplains across hospitals, correctional centres, military commands, corporate organisations, and disaster areas worldwide.

"Service To God, Government & Humanity."

Official Reg: RC 8452727 | RN: SC 251425742
Primary Objective & Core Pillars
To Train and Serve

Equipping chaplains with professional and spiritual toolkits to provide spiritual and emotional care in secular, civil, and high-stress institutions globally.

1. Spiritual Care & Support

Comforting individuals in hospitals, prisons, disaster zones, and high-pressure settings.

2. Training & Equipping

Innovative professional courses for effective chaplaincy in diverse environments.

3. Community Engagement

Fostering fellowship, security, peace, harmony, and humanitarian cooperation.

4. Ethical Standards

Promoting excellence, leadership development, and strict ethical accountability.

5. Global Deployment

Deploying chaplains to all spheres of culture, government, civil defense, and international relief.

Global Evangelism & Missions

Soul Winners' International Outreach (Sowinom)

The Great Commission Discipling Saints Reaching the Unreached

The vision of "SOWINOM" is to obey the Great Commission handed over by Jesus Christ to His disciples as recorded in Matthew 28:19-20, Mark 16:15, Luke 24:47, and Acts 2:38 — preaching Christ and seeing people saved and walking in truth.

"… To preach good tidings unto the meek, He hath sent me to bind up the broken hearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound…… teaching, discipling and equipping the saints on the foundation of the Apostles and teachings of the prophets. That He might be glorified."

— Isaiah 61:1-3
Our Core Mission Pillars
Obey Great Commission

Committed to preaching the whole gospel to every creature and discipling the nations in apostolic truth.

True Worship

Leading men and women in sincere spiritual communion through prayer, hymns, testimonies, and God's Word.

Reach the Unreached

Carrying the light of Christ to overlooked frontiers, marginalized villages, and underserved populations.
